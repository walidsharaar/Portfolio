02 — How I work

My usual process

  1. Understand the business

    Start with the question: which decision should the data support, and who will use it?

  2. Define data requirements

    Agree on sources, KPI definitions and quality rules with the business.

  3. Ingest & store

    Bring data from different systems into one central store and keep the original values.

  4. Model

    Clean, join and structure the data into models that reports can rely on.

  5. Report

    Build dashboards and analyses, and check the numbers with the people who use them.

  6. Review & improve

    Collect feedback, adjust, and start the next round.
